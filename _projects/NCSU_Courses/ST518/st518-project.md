<section id="overview">
  <h2>Project Overview</h2>
  <p>
    This project, completed as part of the <strong>ST518: Applied Statistical Methods II</strong> course at 
    <strong>North Carolina State University</strong>, focuses on the <em>effervescence and solubility behavior</em> 
    of over-the-counter medications under varying chemical and environmental conditions. 
  </p>

  <p>
    Using a designed experiment framework, the analysis examined the effects of factors such as temperature, pH, 
    and reactant concentration on dissolution rate and solubility. Linear and multiple regression models were 
    employed to identify statistically significant predictors of solubility and to quantify the main and interaction effects.
  </p>

  <p>
    Diagnostic plots, residual analysis, and ANOVA summaries were generated to verify model assumptions 
    and assess the strength of relationships among predictors. 
    The findings contribute to a better understanding of formulation efficiency and optimal dissolution conditions 
    in pharmaceutical research.
  </p>
