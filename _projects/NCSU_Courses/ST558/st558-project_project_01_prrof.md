<section id="overview">
  <h2>Project Overview</h2>
  <p>
    This project, completed as part of the <strong>ST558: Data Science for Statisticians</strong> course at 
    <strong>North Carolina State University</strong>, demonstrates the power of the <code>tidyverse</code> 
    suite of R packages in cleaning, transforming, and visualizing data.
  </p>

  <p>
    Using the <em>Census Bureau Statistical Compendia</em> data, I applied 
    <code>dplyr</code>, <code>tidyr</code>, and <code>ggplot2</code> to perform 
    exploratory data analysis (EDA), data wrangling, and visual storytelling. 
    The report showcases reproducible workflows, joining multiple datasets, 
    reshaping data structures, and producing clear visualizations for insight extraction.
  </p>

  </p>
