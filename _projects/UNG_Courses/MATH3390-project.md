<section id="overview">
  <h2>Project Overview</h2>
  <p>
    This project, completed as part of the <strong>MATH 3390: Applied Statistics</strong> course at the 
    <strong>University of North Georgia</strong>, explores the relationship between 
    <em>organic</em> and <em>conventional</em> produce pricing using regression and inferential statistical methods.
  </p>

  <p>
    The analysis investigated whether organic produce prices are significantly higher than their conventional 
    counterparts and examined the economic factors contributing to pricing differentials across multiple produce types.
    The project incorporated descriptive statistics, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, and simple linear 
    regression modeling to identify patterns and price sensitivities within the agricultural market.
  </p>
