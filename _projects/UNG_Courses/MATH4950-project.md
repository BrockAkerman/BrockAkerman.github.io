<section id="overview">
  <h2>Project Overview</h2>
  <p>
    This project, completed as part of the <strong>MATH 4950: Undergraduate Research Capstone</strong> course at the 
    <strong>University of North Georgia</strong>, examines the biological and environmental impacts of 
    <em>deoxygenated river water</em> on <em>rainbow trout propagation</em>.
  </p>

  <p>
    The study explores how varying oxygen levels influence trout population dynamics and reproductive success rates, 
    integrating field data collection with statistical modeling. Using regression analysis and hypothesis testing, 
    the project quantifies the relationship between dissolved oxygen concentration and key biological outcomes such as 
    hatching rate, growth, and survival percentages.
  </p>

  <p>
    Visualizations and summary statistics were developed to communicate the significance of oxygen depletion on 
    aquatic ecosystems, supporting ecological management and conservation strategies.
  </p>

  </p>
